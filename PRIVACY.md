# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Overview

BurauenBiblio is an open-source Integrated Library System (ILS) designed for educational institutions, public libraries, and community libraries.

BurauenBiblio is distributed as self-hosted software. It does not operate as a cloud-hosted service and does not collect information on behalf of the software developers.

Each institution deploying BurauenBiblio is responsible for complying with its applicable privacy and data protection laws.

---

# Privacy by Design

BurauenBiblio follows the principle of **Privacy by Design**, where personal information is processed only to support legitimate library operations.

The software is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and allows institutions to retain full control over their own information systems.

---

# Personal Information Processed

Depending on how an institution configures the software, BurauenBiblio may store information such as:

- Patron name
- Library identification number
- Student or employee number
- Address
- Contact number
- Email address
- Department or course 
- Borrower type (Student or Faculty)
- Borrowing history
- Book reservation history
- Circulation records
- Account status
- Library fines and payments (if enabled)

Institutions may customize which fields are collected according to their operational policies.

---

# Purpose of Collection

Personal information is processed solely for legitimate library functions, including:

- Patron registration
- Borrowing and returning library materials
- Reservation management
- Overdue notifications
- Inventory management
- Statistical reporting
- Library administration
- Compliance with institutional policies

Personal information should not be collected for purposes unrelated to library operations.

---

# Data Ownership

BurauenBiblio does **not** own patron data.

All personal information stored within a BurauenBiblio installation remains the property and responsibility of the institution operating the software.

The software developers do not receive copies of institutional databases.

---

# No Automatic Data Collection

BurauenBiblio does **not**:

- transmit patron information to the developers
- upload library databases to external servers
- perform analytics using patron data
- include advertising trackers
- include third-party telemetry
- sell personal information
- share patron information with third parties

Unless separately configured by the institution, BurauenBiblio communicates only within the institution's own infrastructure.

---

# Data Security

Institutions are encouraged to implement appropriate security measures, including:

- Strong administrator passwords
- Role-based user accounts
- HTTPS encryption
- Regular software updates
- Routine database backups
- Secure server configurations
- Firewall protection
- Access logging where appropriate

The security of personal information ultimately depends on the deployment and operational practices of each institution.

---

# Data Retention

Library records should be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill institutional, legal, and operational requirements.

Retention periods should follow the policies established by the institution and applicable laws.

---

# Disclosure of Information

BurauenBiblio does not disclose patron information.

Institutions operating the software are responsible for determining when disclosure is legally required, such as:

- lawful court orders
- authorized government requests
- institutional disciplinary procedures
- other situations permitted or required by applicable law

---

# Open Source Transparency

Because BurauenBiblio is open-source software, its source code is publicly available for inspection.

This transparency allows institutions to verify how personal information is processed and supports independent security reviews.

---

# Philippine Data Privacy Compliance

For institutions operating within the Republic of the Philippines, BurauenBiblio is intended to support compliance with:

- Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of the Data Privacy Act
- National Privacy Commission (NPC) issuances and guidelines

The institution deploying BurauenBiblio acts as the **Personal Information Controller (PIC)** and is responsible for complying with applicable legal obligations under Philippine law.

---

# International Compliance

Institutions outside the Philippines should comply with their respective privacy and data protection regulations, including but not limited to:

- GDPR (European Union)
- UK GDPR
- CCPA/CPRA (California)
- Other applicable national or regional privacy laws

BurauenBiblio is designed to support institution-controlled deployments without mandatory cloud-based processing.

---

# Third-Party Services

BurauenBiblio does not require third-party analytics or cloud services for normal operation.

If an institution integrates external services (such as email providers, authentication systems, cloud backups, or AI services), the institution is responsible for evaluating the privacy implications of those integrations.

---

# Contact

Questions regarding institutional data should be directed to the library or institution operating the BurauenBiblio installation.

Questions regarding the BurauenBiblio software project may be submitted through the project's GitHub Issues page.
